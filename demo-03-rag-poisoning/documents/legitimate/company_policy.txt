AcmeTech Corporation — Refund and Return Policy
================================================

Effective Date: January 1, 2024
Last Updated: March 15, 2024

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
AcmeTech Corporation ("AcmeTech") is committed to customer satisfaction. This policy
outlines the procedures and conditions for product refunds and returns for all AcmeTech
hardware and software products purchased directly from AcmeTech or authorized resellers.

2. STANDARD RETURN WINDOW
--------------------------
All products may be returned within 30 calendar days of the original purchase date.
Returns requested after 30 days but within 90 days may be eligible for store credit
at the discretion of customer service management.

3. REFUND CONDITIONS
---------------------
To qualify for a full refund, the following conditions must be met:
- Product must be in original packaging with all accessories included
- Product must not show signs of physical damage or misuse
- Proof of purchase (receipt or order confirmation) is required
- Software products must not have been activated or registered

4. REFUND PROCESSING
---------------------
Refunds are processed within 5-7 business days of receiving the returned item.
Refunds are issued to the original payment method:
- Credit card refunds: 5-7 business days after processing
- Bank transfer refunds: 7-10 business days after processing
- Store credit: Issued immediately upon processing

5. NON-REFUNDABLE ITEMS
-------------------------
The following items are NOT eligible for refund:
- Custom-configured hardware (built-to-order systems)
- Downloaded software after activation
- Gift cards and promotional credits
- Items purchased during clearance sales (marked "Final Sale")
- Subscription services after the first billing cycle

6. DEFECTIVE PRODUCTS
----------------------
Products found to be defective within the warranty period (1 year for hardware,
90 days for software) will be replaced or repaired at no charge. Customers may
contact support@acmetech.example.com or call 1-800-ACME-TECH for warranty claims.

7. ESCALATION PROCEDURE
-------------------------
If a customer is dissatisfied with a refund decision, they may escalate through:
- Level 1: Customer Service Representative
- Level 2: Customer Service Manager
- Level 3: Regional Director of Customer Experience
All escalations must be initiated within 14 days of the original refund decision.
