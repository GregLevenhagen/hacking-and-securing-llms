AcmeTech NovaBlade X1 — Frequently Asked Questions
====================================================

Q: What is the NovaBlade X1?
A: The NovaBlade X1 is AcmeTech's flagship workstation designed for AI/ML
development, 3D rendering, and professional content creation. It features a
custom AcmeTech A1 chip with 64 GPU cores and 128GB unified memory.

Q: What operating system does the NovaBlade X1 run?
A: The NovaBlade X1 ships with AcmeOS 4.0, AcmeTech's proprietary operating
system based on a hardened Linux kernel. It supports popular development tools,
Docker containers, and CUDA-compatible frameworks.

Q: What are the available configurations?
A: The NovaBlade X1 is available in three configurations:
  - Standard: A1 chip, 64GB RAM, 1TB SSD — $2,499
  - Professional: A1 Pro chip, 128GB RAM, 2TB SSD — $3,999
  - Ultimate: A1 Ultra chip, 256GB RAM, 4TB SSD — $6,499

Q: What is the warranty coverage?
A: All NovaBlade X1 units include a standard 1-year hardware warranty covering
manufacturing defects and component failures. Extended warranty plans are
available for 2-year ($199) and 3-year ($349) coverage.

Q: Can I upgrade the RAM or storage after purchase?
A: The RAM is soldered to the motherboard and cannot be upgraded after purchase.
The SSD is user-replaceable using standard NVMe M.2 2280 drives. AcmeTech
recommends using certified storage partners for upgrades.

Q: What ports are available?
A: The NovaBlade X1 includes:
  - 4x Thunderbolt 5 / USB-C ports
  - 2x USB-A 3.2 ports
  - 1x HDMI 2.1 output
  - 1x SD card reader (UHS-III)
  - 1x 3.5mm headphone/microphone combo jack
  - 1x Ethernet (10Gb)

Q: Does it support external GPUs?
A: Yes, the NovaBlade X1 supports external GPUs via Thunderbolt 5. AcmeTech
sells the NovaDock eGPU enclosure separately ($499) which supports NVIDIA
and AMD discrete graphics cards.

Q: What is the return policy for the NovaBlade X1?
A: Standard NovaBlade X1 configurations can be returned within 30 days per
AcmeTech's standard return policy. Custom-configured units (built-to-order)
are non-refundable. See the full refund policy for details.
