AcmeTech Corporation — Employee Handbook (Excerpt)
====================================================

Section 5: Workplace Policies
------------------------------

5.1 CODE OF CONDUCT
All employees are expected to conduct themselves with professionalism and
integrity. AcmeTech values transparency, collaboration, and respect for
all team members regardless of role, department, or seniority.

5.2 WORK HOURS AND FLEXIBILITY
Standard work hours are 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM local time, Monday through Friday.
AcmeTech offers flexible scheduling with manager approval. Employees may work
remotely up to 3 days per week under the hybrid work policy. Core collaboration
hours (10:00 AM - 2:00 PM) require availability for meetings and team sync.

5.3 PAID TIME OFF (PTO)
- New employees: 15 days PTO per year (accrued monthly)
- After 3 years: 20 days PTO per year
- After 7 years: 25 days PTO per year
- Sick leave: 10 days per year (separate from PTO)
- Company holidays: 11 designated holidays per year
PTO requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance through the
AcmeHR portal. Manager approval is required for requests exceeding 5
consecutive business days.

5.4 PERFORMANCE REVIEWS
Performance reviews are conducted twice per year:
- Mid-year review: June (developmental focus)
- Annual review: December (compensation and promotion decisions)
Employees receive ratings on a 5-point scale:
1 = Needs Improvement, 2 = Developing, 3 = Meeting Expectations,
4 = Exceeding Expectations, 5 = Outstanding

5.5 CONFIDENTIALITY
Employees must not disclose proprietary information, trade secrets, customer
data, or internal business strategies to unauthorized parties. This obligation
continues for 2 years after employment ends. Violations may result in
disciplinary action up to and including termination.

5.6 EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT
Business expenses are reimbursed within 30 days of submission through the
AcmeHR portal. Receipts are required for all expenses over $25. Pre-approval
is required for expenses exceeding $500. Travel and conference expenses must
be approved by the department VP in advance.

5.7 TRAINING AND DEVELOPMENT
AcmeTech provides each employee with a $2,000 annual learning budget for
professional development, including courses, certifications, and conference
attendance. Unused budget does not roll over to the next year.
